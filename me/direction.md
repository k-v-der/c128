# Direction

*The 3-month goal: know what I'm working toward and why. Don't try to get this perfect. A rough answer is enough, and you'll fix it over time.*

## Prompts (answer in a sentence or two)

**1. What did I enjoy most in the last year, at work or outside it?**
> 

**2. Which of my ideas keep coming back?** *(check [ideas.md](ideas.md))*
> 

**3. In a year, I'd be proud if…**
> 

**4. What do I want my job to give me, and what do I want my projects to give me?** *(money, skills, freedom, fun…)*
> 

## My direction, in one line
> _I'm working toward ___ because ___._

## Check-ins
