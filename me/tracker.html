The rules

  1. Food and bills are set aside first. Ads never touch them.
  2. Ads get a fixed budget for the month. When it's spent, stop.
  3. When a product has used its test budget and still loses money, stop it. No "one more day".
  4. Count delivered orders. Refused orders still cost you.
